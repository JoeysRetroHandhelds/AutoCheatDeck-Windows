## Fixes

**Launching from Steam (as a non-Steam game) no longer drops you back into Steam.** With **Start with Windows** on, AutoCheatDeck is already running in the tray, so a launch from Steam just showed that copy's window and exited at once. Steam took that as the game closing and covered AutoCheatDeck with its own fullscreen UI. Now the launch stays until you close AutoCheatDeck's window (back to the tray), so Steam sees it running the whole time. It sleeps while it waits and uses no CPU.

**Clearer logs.** The log now records when another launch shows the window.
